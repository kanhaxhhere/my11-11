A letter for you 💌

Dear Anwesa,

I wanted to make something that feels like you: warm like your sunsets, sweet like Bengal, and full of the kind of joy that makes everyone around you smile.

You have a way of looking at the sky and finding beauty where most people just walk past. I hope you know that you do the same with people. You make the ordinary feel special.

And that 11:11... every time I see it on a clock, I think of you. It feels like a little wish that came true. ✨

Thank you for being you, and for being in my life. Whatever comes next, I want to keep making you smile.

Always yours,
Anand 💗
